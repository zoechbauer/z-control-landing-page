3. Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics, an analysis service from Google, on this website to statistically evaluate usage of our website and offered applications and to continuously improve our offerings.

This includes processing information about which functions or areas are used, which events are triggered in our applications, as well as technical conditions under which the website or applications are used (e.g., device, browser, operating system, language, approximate location by country, and technical identifiers).

4. Activation of Firebase Analytics

Data collection via Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. When loading the website, data collection by Analytics is expressly deactivated.

Your own Analytics events are only transmitted to Firebase after you activate Analytics collection via the provided setting.

Activation is voluntary but necessary to fully use all features offered on the landing page.

When Analytics is disabled, no custom Analytics events are transmitted to Firebase. The technical control is performed via the Firebase function setAnalyticsCollectionEnabled().

Regardless of Analytics activation, daily aggregated visit counts and an anonymized user identifier are recorded in a separate Firebase/Firestore database. This serves to analyze usage and estimate how many visitors have activated Firebase Analytics.

Additionally, at disabled Analytics, daily summaries of certain interactions with the landing page (such as viewing the privacy policy or changing the language) are recorded anonymously.

The collected data are used exclusively for statistical evaluation, improving user-friendliness, and further development of the landing page.

5. Purpose of Processing

The processing using Firebase Analytics serves primarily the following purposes:

  • Analysis of website and application usage;
  • Identification of frequently used functions and areas;
  • Improvement of user-friendliness;
  • Detection and correction of technical or functional issues;
  • Development of existing and new features;
  • Statistical evaluation of usage.

Additionally, aggregated daily usage data and anonymized user information are processed via the separate Firebase/Firestore service to:

  • Record daily landing page visits;
  • Differ between visits with and without activated Firebase Analytics;
  • Estimate if aggregated data comes from many or few different anonymized users;
  • Evaluate use of the privacy policy and language function when Analytics is disabled;
  • Improve user-friendliness and further develop the landing page.

6. Advertising and Marketing

We do not use Google Ads integration, Google Signals, or other Google Analytics advertising features.

Our applications and tools are offered free of charge and are not funded through advertising or personalized marketing based on Firebase Analytics data.

11. Revocation and Deactivation

You may revoke consent for Firebase Analytics at any time or disable Analytics data collection. After deactivation, no further Analytics events will be transmitted to Firebase through our application.

The separate collection of landing page statistics and anonymized user information described in section 4 remains unaffected as it is based on a separate Firebase/Firestore service.

Data already processed by Google or Firebase will not be automatically deleted upon revocation. Processing and storage follow Google Analytics/Firebase policies.

12. Storage Duration

The retention period of Analytics data depends on the configured retention settings in Google Analytics/Firebase and applicable Google policies.

Aggregated daily usage data and anonymized user information stored in the separate Firebase/Firestore service are currently kept without a fixed time limit. Regular automatic deletion is not currently planned.

The anonymized user information is exclusively used to better interpret aggregated usage data regarding the number of different anonymized users and is not used to create personal profiles.

We do not store personal profiles of visitors ourselves and do not require registration for using the landing page.
